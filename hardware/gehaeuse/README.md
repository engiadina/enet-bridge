# Housing / Gehäuse

3D-printable housing for the eNet Bridge (ESP32 DevKit + CC1101 module, 868 MHz rubber antenna on an SMA cable).
Photo and description: [docs/HARDWARE.md](../../docs/HARDWARE.md#housing).

| File | Part | Size (mm) |
|---|---|---|
| [`gehaeuse.stl`](gehaeuse.stl) | housing with antenna socket on top | 80 × 80 × 95 |
| [`boden.stl`](boden.stl) | base with insert holding the circuit boards | 74 × 74 × 58 |
| [`antennenhalter.stl`](antennenhalter.stl) | holder for the SMA socket of the antenna | 17 × 12 × 17 |

Inside, the ESP32 and the radio module are held with hot glue – a better construction may follow.
